Usage: node scripts/rain/story-smoke.mjs --output <new-directory> [--concurrency <1|2|3>] [--plan]
The output parent must exist. Existing output paths are rejected. Default concurrency: 1.
--help and --plan do not fetch pages, load Playwright, or create scan output.
STORYBOOK_URL keeps its existing meaning (default http://127.0.0.1:6006).
